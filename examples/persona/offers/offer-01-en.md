# Senior Backend Engineer, Fabrikam Logistics

Fabrikam Logistics runs parcel tracking for thousands of shops. We are looking for a senior backend engineer to join the platform team. You will work with our engineers and build services for our customers.

What you will do
- Build and run backend services in TypeScript and NestJS.
- Process parcel events with Kafka.
- Own PostgreSQL schemas and keep queries fast.
- Run services on Kubernetes in production.

What we ask
- Experience with TypeScript and NestJS.
- Experience with Kafka or a similar message broker.
- Solid PostgreSQL skills.
- Kubernetes experience in production.

Nice to have
- Terraform.
- Redis.
- Mentoring other engineers.
